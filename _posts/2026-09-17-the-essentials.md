The Big Four 



* Backpacking Pack — Internal-frame pack (45–55L) fitted to distribute weight to your hips
* Shelter — Tent, tarp, or hammock with stakes and rainfly for weather and bugs
* Sleeping Bag or Quilt — Rated at least 10°F below your coldest expected night
* Sleeping Pad — Insulated pad between you and the ground — check the ASTM R-value



Food & Hydration



* Water Filtration & Storage — Filter, chemical treatment, or UV purifier, plus bottles or a bladder
* Camp Kitchen & Stove — Stove, fuel, lightweight pot, and a spoon or spork
* Nutrition & Extra Food — High-calorie meals and snacks, plus one extra day's food for emergencies
* Food Storage Protection — Bear canister, Ursack, or hang bag to keep wildlife out of your food



Safety & Trail Essentials



* Navigation — Map and compass, plus an offline app (like AllTrails) or GPS unit
* Illumination — Headlamp plus spare batteries or a backup charger
* First Aid & Repair Kit — Personal med kit, plus a mini-tool, knife, and duct tape
* Clothing Layers — Moisture-wicking base layer, insulating fleece or down, and a rain shell
